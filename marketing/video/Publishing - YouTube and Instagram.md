# Publishing: YouTube & Instagram

Copy-paste text for every video. Thumbnails and covers are in `thumbnails/`; rebuild them with `node make_thumbnails.js` after editing `thumbnails.html`.

**Every link points to `https://injurycase.com/start`** (it redirects to the free-trial sign-up, so it can be retargeted later without editing any post).

**Keyword note (vidIQ, Oct 2026):** buyer phrases like "personal injury chiropractic software" get fewer than 750 searches a month, while big terms ("whiplash", "car accident chiropractor") are searched by patients, not clinics. Titles therefore lead with the plain buyer phrase that clinic owners search for. Tags mix the exact niche terms with a few broad ones so YouTube understands the topic.

**Wording rules used throughout:** "HIPAA-ready" (not "HIPAA certified" or "HIPAA compliant"), "educational tool / clinical decisions stay with the treating provider", and "demo data is fictional".
