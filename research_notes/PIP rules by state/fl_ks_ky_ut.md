## Q1. Florida baseline: are the app's current FL assumptions still correct in late 2026?

### Takeaway
Yes. Florida's no-fault/PIP system is still in force in Oct 2026: $10k PIP, the 14-day rule, the EMC split between $10k and $2,500, and 80% of a fee schedule set at 200% of Medicare Part B. Every repeal bill (2021 vetoed; 2025 HB 1181 withdrawn; 2026 SB 522 / HB 769 died in committee on 13 Mar 2026) failed. The app may be missing several FL details: who may make an EMC determination (chiropractors may not), the March-1 Medicare-year rule with its 2007 floor, the 35-day billing window, the pre-suit demand letter (627.736(10)) with its 30-day cure period, and the 2023 HB 837 letter-of-protection disclosure rules (768.0427).

### Cited Findings
**Repeal status**
- 2026 session ended 13 Mar 2026 with no repeal. SB 522 died in Senate Banking & Insurance and HB 769 died in House Civil Justice & Claims Subcommittee. Both would have repealed no-fault effective 1 Jan 2027 and moved FL to a fault system with 25/50/10 minimum BI/PD. — [Claims Journal, 6 May 2026](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/05/06/337365.htm); [hov.law](https://www.hov.law/learn/did-florida-repeal-pip-2026); [Atesa Risk Advisors](https://atesariskadvisors.com/blog/florida-pip-not-repealed-2026)
- A repeal bill passed both chambers only once, in 2021, and Gov. DeSantis vetoed it. 2025 HB 1181 (repeal "effective July 1, 2026") was withdrawn on 3 May 2025. This bill is the source of widespread misinformation that PIP was repealed on 1 Jul 2026. — [Claims Journal 2026](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/05/06/337365.htm); [Viles & Beckman](https://www.vilesandbeckman.com/blog/did-florida-repeal-no-fault-insurance-the-pip-myths-and-the-facts-for-2026/); [SandStone Insurance](https://sandstoneins.com/blog/florida-no-fault-car-insurance-in-2026-did-the-law-actually-change/)
- The next regular session is in 2027, so watch for refiled repeal bills. — [hov.law](https://www.hov.law/learn/did-florida-repeal-pip-2026)

**Benefits / EMC (Fla. Stat. 627.736(1)(a))**
- $10,000 PIP. Initial services must be received within 14 days of the accident. — [Graham & Syfert summary of 627.736](https://www.grahamsyfert.com/florida-statutes/section-627-736.html); [Transcure](https://transcure.net/medical-billing/florida-laws-pip/)
- An EMC determination may be made only by: a physician licensed under ch. 458 or 459 (MD/DO), a dentist (ch. 466), a physician assistant (ch. 458/459), or an APRN (ch. 464). A chiropractic physician may provide initial PIP care but **cannot** determine an EMC. — [Furmanski Law Group](https://furmanskilawgroup.com/what-is-an-emergency-medical-condition-under-floridas-pip-law/); [Transcure](https://transcure.net/medical-billing/florida-laws-pip/); [Citimed](https://citimed.com/emergency-medical-condition-determination-florida/)
- With an EMC determination, the cap is up to $10,000. With a "no EMC" determination, the cap is $2,500. An appellate ruling (quoted by Dolman Law) says that with **no determination at all** benefits are also limited to $2,500. Courts have not treated the "no determination" case uniformly. — [Dolman Law](https://www.dolmanlaw.com/blog/new-personal-injury-protection-pip-ruling-emergency-medical-condition-effects-medical-practice/); [JMM Law](https://jmmlawfirm.com/what-are-emc-designations/)

**Fee schedule / reimbursement (627.736(5)(a))**
- The insurer may pay 80% of 200% of the Medicare Part B participating physicians fee schedule for most services. — [Transcure](https://transcure.net/medical-billing/florida-laws-pip/); [MediClaim Solutions](https://mediclaimsolutions.com/blog/florida-pip-fee-schedule-billing-guide)
- **Medicare year:** the applicable schedule is the one in effect on **March 1 of the year of service**, for the area where care was given. Later changes that year do not apply. The amount may not be less than the **2007** schedule. The insurer may apply the limit only if the policy gave notice of it at issuance or renewal. — [flsenate.gov 2024 text of 627.736 (via search extract)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/statutes/2024/627.736); [CSK Legal](https://www.csklegal.com/newsroom/publications/pip-and-the-use-of-the-medicare-fee-schedule-flag-on-the-play)
- Florida's 3rd DCA (2021) held that the 2007 **non-facility limiting charge** was the proper floor rate under the post-2012 "applicable schedule" wording. Billing vendors simplify this to "200% of participating," which is not quite the same. — [Conroy Simberg](https://www.conroysimberg.com/blog/april-28-2021-third-district-court-of-appeal-issues-important-ruling-on-determining-the-proper-medicare-fee-schedule-for-purpose-of-pip-reimbursement/)

**Billing deadline**
- Most providers must submit bills within **35 days** of service. — [Transcure](https://transcure.net/medical-billing/florida-laws-pip/)

**Demand letter (627.736(10))**
- A demand letter is a condition precedent to suit. It may not be sent until the claim is overdue. It must state that it is a "demand letter under s. 627.736" and give specifics: insured name, claim or policy number, and an itemized statement of each amount claimed, with dates of service and benefit type. The insurer then has **30 days** to pay or deny. Courts differ on whether strict or substantial compliance is required. — [flsenate.gov 627.736](https://www.flsenate.gov/laws/statutes/2024/627.736); [Claims Journal 2016](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/southeast/2016/02/24/269035.htm); [The Injury Firm](https://flinjuryfirm.com/personal-injury-lawyers/newsworthy/demand-letter-compliance); [Avvo guide](https://www.avvo.com/legal-guides/ugc/pip-demand-letter-a-condition-precedent-is-substantial-compliance-enough-)
- Insurers commonly use 627.736(6)(b) requests for treatment records and history. — [LegalGiant](https://legalgiant.co/what-is-a-627-7366b-request-in-florida/)

**Letters of protection (HB 837, 2023; Fla. Stat. 768.0427)**
- In a PI case under a letter of protection, 768.0427(3) requires disclosure of the LOP arrangement. Admissible evidence of past medical expenses is limited to amounts actually paid or owed (768.0427(2)). HB 837 also reformed bad faith (624.155) and one-way attorney fees. — [floridacourtrules.com](https://floridacourtrules.com/florida-car-accident/)
- 2025 HB 1437 (House analysis, 20 Mar 2025) proposed changes to attorney fees in provider suits over overdue PIP. No source confirmed whether it passed. — [FL House analysis h1437a](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/1437/Analyses/h1437a.CIV.PDF)

### Inferences
- The app should store the EMC determination as structured data: provider license type (MD, DO, DMD/DDS, PA or APRN), date, and positive or negative result. It should block a chiropractor (DC) from being recorded as the EMC determiner, and it should handle the "no determination yet" state as a $2,500 exposure.
- The fee engine needs: date of service, then the Medicare Part B schedule in effect on 1 March of that year for the locality, times 2.0. Compare that with the 2007 floor (non-facility limiting charge, per the 3rd DCA) and take the higher figure. Then multiply by 0.80, which is the insurer's share. Also add a flag for whether the policy contains the fee-schedule notice.
- Add task timers: 14-day initial-care deadline, 35-day bill submission, a demand letter allowed only once the claim is overdue, and a 30-day cure countdown.

## Q2. Kansas (K.S.A. 40-3101 et seq., Kansas Automobile Injury Reparations Act)

### Takeaway
Kansas PIP medical minimum is **$4,500**, with a separate **$4,500 rehabilitation** benefit and disability of **$900/month for up to 1 year**. Benefits are overdue after **30 days** from written notice of the loss and its amount, and then bear **18% simple interest**. The tort threshold (40-3117) is **$2,000 reasonable value of medical treatment** or listed serious injuries. There is **no state PIP fee schedule**; the standard is reasonable and customary charges. Kansas's statutory lien (K.S.A. 65-406) covers **hospitals and certain clinics, not chiropractors**.

### Cited Findings
- K.S.A. 40-3103: medical benefits up to a limit of not less than $4,500. Rehabilitation (psychiatric/psychological, occupational therapy, retraining) not less than $4,500. Disability up to not less than $900/month for not more than 1 year. — [ksrevisor 40-3103](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch40/040_031_0003.html); [Justia 2025 K.S.A. 40-3103](https://law.justia.com/codes/kansas/chapter-40/article-31/section-40-3103/)
- PIP medical covers reasonable and necessary care, including chiropractors. — [Kansas City Accident Injury Attorneys](https://www.kansascityaccidentinjuryattorneys.com/library/personal-injury-protection-in-ks-pip-coverage-requirements/); [Kevin McManus Law](https://www.kevinmcmanuslaw.com/faqs/how-to-pay-chiropractor-bills-after-car-accident-kansas-city/)
- K.S.A. 40-3110: benefits are due as loss accrues once reasonable proof is received. They are overdue if not paid within **30 days** after written notice of a covered loss and its amount. Disability is paid at least every two weeks. Overdue payments bear **18% per annum simple interest**. Annotations note no interest penalty where a good-faith controversy existed. — [ksrevisor 40-3110](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch40/040_031_0010.html); [ksrevisor annotations](http://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/annos/ch40/040_031_0001_annos.html)
- K.S.A. 40-3117 tort threshold for pain and suffering and other non-pecuniary damages: the injury must require medical treatment with a **reasonable value of $2,000 or more**, or consist in whole or in part of permanent disfigurement, a fracture to a weight-bearing bone, or other listed categories. Charges actually made are **not conclusive** of reasonable value. — [ksrevisor 40-3117](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch40/040_031_0017.html); [2024 K.S.A. 40-3117 PDF](https://www.kslegislature.gov/li_2024/m/statute/040_000_0000_chapter/040_031_0000_article/040_031_0017_section/040_031_0017_k.pdf)
- The threshold does not bar suits for economic damages such as medical bills and lost wages; it applies only to non-pecuniary damages. — [Sloan Law Firm](https://www.sloanlawfirm.com/kansas-no-fault-personal-injury-protection/)
- No state-mandated auto/PIP fee schedule exists in Kansas. The standard is reasonable and necessary / usual and customary. The Kansas workers' comp fee schedule is separate (KDOL). — [AC Billing, Kansas](https://acbilling.com/chiropractic-billing-kansas/); [KDOL WC fee schedule](https://www.dol.ks.gov/workers-compensation/medical-services-fee-schedule)
- K.S.A. 65-406 to 65-409: the lien belongs to hospitals and certain qualifying clinics. The first $5,000 of the lien is enforceable; any amount above that is enforceable only to the extent it is an equitable distribution. A payer that settles after proper notice without satisfying the lien remains liable to the hospital for 1 year (65-408). — [Joe's Coffee Break summary](https://www.joescoffeebreak.com/2022/11/does-medical-provider-get-part-of-the-personal-injury-settlement/); [House Law KC](https://www.houselawkc.com/liens-personal-injury-case/); [ksrevisor 65-408](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch65/065_004_0008.html)
- A secondary source says PIP is subrogable only if the collision occurs in Kansas (40-3113a). This could not be verified against the statute. — [Kevin McManus Law](https://www.kevinmcmanuslaw.com/faqs/how-to-use-med-pay-and-pip-for-car-accidents/)

### Inferences
- The app needs a separate KS "rehab" bucket next to "medical" because the two are distinct $4,500 limits. Chiropractic normally draws from the medical bucket.
- For a typical chiropractic course, the $2,000 threshold is often reached. Track a running "reasonable value" total separately from billed charges, because billed charges are not conclusive.
- Without a statutory chiropractic lien, KS clinics depend on contractual assignment of benefits, LOPs and attorney acknowledgment letters.

### Gaps
- No Kansas source was found for a provider bill-submission deadline, required PIP claim form, utilization review/IME statute, or chiropractic visit limits. **[UNVERIFIED]** Background knowledge: Kansas has no statutory provider billing deadline; insurers may require IMEs under policy terms (K.S.A. 40-3111?). Needs verification.
- The full list of serious injuries in the 40-3117 threshold could not be retrieved, nor any exclusions (e.g., for diagnostic X-ray costs) when computing the $2,000. **[UNVERIFIED]** Background knowledge: older text excluded diagnostic X-ray and rehab costs from the computation and listed permanent injury, permanent loss of a body function, and death.
- No Kansas PIP reforms for 2019–2026 were found.
- Arbitration: **[UNVERIFIED]** Background knowledge: K.S.A. 40-3113a provides arbitration for PIP reimbursement disputes between insurers. Not confirmed.

## Q3. Kentucky (KRS 304.39, Motor Vehicle Reparations Act) — major 2026 reform

### Takeaway
Kentucky BRB (PIP) is still **$10,000**. **HB 627 (2026), Acts ch. 149**, became law **without the Governor's signature on 14 Apr 2026** and took effect about **15 Jul 2026**. It changes the rules materially for policies issued or renewed after the effective date:
- weekly wage-loss cap rises from $200 to $500
- funeral benefit rises from $1,000 to $5,000
- medical payments are tied to the **KY workers' comp fee schedule (KRS 342.035)**
- **balance billing is barred**
- the **180-day bill-submission deadline** stays in place
- the presumption that submitted bills are reasonable is removed
- the Attorney General gains authority over fraud

Overdue benefits: **30 days** after reasonable proof, interest at **12%**, or **18%** if the delay was without reasonable foundation. Tort threshold is **>$1,000 medical** or a listed injury, unless the person rejected the tort limitation (KRS 304.39-060).

### Cited Findings
**HB 627 / Acts 2026 ch. 149**
- Passed House 85-9 and Senate 24-8. Became law without the Governor's signature on 14 Apr 2026 (Acts ch. 149). — [KY Legislature 26RS HB 627 record](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/record/26rs/hb627.html); [Acts ch. 149 PDF](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/acts/26RS/documents/0149.pdf); [enrolled bill](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/recorddocuments/bill/26RS/hb627/bill.pdf)
- Took effect around 15 Jul 2026, 90 days after adjournment. Enhanced benefits apply to policies issued or renewed after that date. Crashes under older policies may fall under the old rules. — [Garmer & Prather, 14 Jul 2026](https://www.garmerprather.com/blog/kentuckys-new-pip-law/); [BBO Law](https://bbo-law.com/blog/kentucky-pip-law-hb627-what-it-means-for-your-accident-claim/); [HF KY Lawyers, Jul 2026](https://www.hfkylawyers.com/blog/2026/07/what-kentuckys-new-pip-law-means-for-your-claim/)
- Weekly cap rises from $200 to $500 (KRS 304.39-130). Funeral benefit rises from $1,000 to $5,000. The $10,000 overall limit is unchanged. — [Aguiar Injury Lawyers](https://aguiarinjurylawyers.com/kentucky-pip-reform-2026/); [Richard Breen Law](https://richardbreen.com/news/car-accident/did-kentuckys-no-fault-pip-law-change-louisville-car-accident-lawyer/); [HF KY Lawyers](https://www.hfkylawyers.com/blog/2026/07/what-kentuckys-new-pip-law-means-for-your-claim/)
- Medical expense payment under amended KRS 304.39-210 is "at the greater of" (1) the KY workers' comp fee-schedule amount under KRS 342.035 or (2) a contracted rate (per the HB 416 (2025) bill text, which carried the same concept). Sources conflict on whether the WC schedule works as a **cap** (law-firm summaries) or a **floor/greater-of** (bill text, Senate summary). — [HB 416 (2025) text](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/recorddocuments/bill/25RS/hb416/bill.pdf); [Citizen Portal Senate summary](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/7934392/Kentucky/2026-Legislature-KY/Senate-approves-changes-to-PIP-insurance-claims-billing-and-fraud-enforcement-in-House-Bill-627); [Aguiar HB627 resource](https://aguiarinjurylawyers.com/resources/kentucky-pip-reform-hb627/)
- Current KRS 304.39-210 also says the obligor shall not pay a charge exceeding the maximum fee permitted under another KRS 304.39 section (cut off in the extract). — [KRS 304.39-210](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57856)
- Providers are barred from balance billing patients for PIP claims. Bills must generally be submitted within **180 days** of service. The presumption that submitted bills are reasonable is removed. The AG gets concurrent fraud-prosecution authority. The KY Chiropractic Association and KY PT Association reportedly did not oppose the final compromise. — [Aguiar](https://aguiarinjurylawyers.com/kentucky-pip-reform-2026/); [HF KY Lawyers](https://www.hfkylawyers.com/blog/2026/07/what-kentuckys-new-pip-law-means-for-your-claim/)

**Payment, direction of benefits**
- KRS 304.39-210: benefits are overdue if not paid within **30 days** after the obligor receives reasonable proof of the fact and amount of loss. The obligor may instead accumulate claims for periods of up to 31 days and pay within 15 days after the period ends. Overdue amounts bear **12%** per annum interest, or **18%** if the delay was without reasonable foundation. Medical benefits may be paid directly to providers if the claimant designates it. — [KRS 304.39-210](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57856)
- KRS 304.39-241: the insured may direct, in writing, how benefits are allocated among elements of loss. The obligor must honor the direction prospectively from receipt. — [HB 416 text](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/recorddocuments/bill/25RS/hb416/bill.pdf); [KRS ch. 304 subtitle 39 index](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/chapter.aspx?id=38757)
- KRS 304.39-245 (catchline): the obligor may request or negotiate a reduction or modification of charges. — [KRS index](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/chapter.aspx?id=38757)

**Tort threshold / rejection (KRS 304.39-060)**
- Non-economic damages are recoverable only if PIP-payable medical expenses exceed **$1,000**, or the injury involves a bone fracture, permanent disfigurement, permanent injury, permanent loss of bodily function, or death. A person with free medical care meets the threshold if the equivalent value is at least $1,000. The tort limitation does not apply to a person who has formally rejected it. — [KRS 304.39-060](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?amp=&id=30030); [Justia 2025 KRS 304.39-060](https://law.justia.com/codes/kentucky/chapter-304/subtitle-304-39/section-304-39-060/); [Debra Broz Law, Aug 2026](https://debrabrozlaw.com/blog/2026/08/kentucky-pip-insurance-lawsuit-thresholds/)

**Liens**
- Kentucky is listed as having no statewide hospital or provider lien statute (MWL chart, updated Jan 2022). — [MWL 50-state hospital lien chart](https://www.mwl-law.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/HOSPITAL-LIEN-LAWS-IN-ALL-50-STATES-CHART-00215685x9EBBF.pdf)

### Inferences
- The KY configuration needs **policy-date versioning**: issue or renewal date before or after about 15 Jul 2026 drives the wage cap, funeral benefit, fee-schedule rules and the balance-billing ban.
- Add a 180-day bill-submission timer per date of service, a fee lookup against the KY WC fee schedule (KRS 342.035 / 803 KAR 25:089), and a "no balance billing of PIP-covered services" flag.
- Track any written direction of benefits by the insured, since it can cut off or reserve BRB for chiropractic bills. Also store whether the patient rejected the tort limitation, which changes the threshold logic.
- With no statutory lien, KY clinics rely on assignments and LOPs.

Unconfirmed: whether the fee schedule is a cap or a floor, the exact balance-billing language, whether a 2026 change to the 180-day rule exists, utilization review and peer review provisions, and the effective date (law firms say about 15 Jul 2026).

## Q4. Utah (Utah Code 31A-22-307 to -309)

### Takeaway
Utah PIP medical minimum is **$3,000 per person**. Lost income is the lesser of $250/week or 85% for up to 52 weeks, plus household services and funeral benefits. Benefits are overdue **30 days** after reasonable proof and then bear **1.5% per month** interest plus attorney fees if the insurer is forced to pay. The tort threshold is **medical expenses over $3,000** or listed injuries, including bone fracture. One secondary source says Utah has **no state PIP fee schedule**. The statute does reference a commissioner-run **relative value study** for setting reasonable medical charges. 31A-22-309 was amended in the 2026 session (ch. 382), and the earlier version is marked "superseded 5/6/2026".

### Cited Findings
- 31A-22-307: covers the reasonable value of necessary medical, surgical and related services, minimum **$3,000/person**. Lost income is the lesser of $250/week or 85% of income, up to 52 weeks. Also covers a household-services allowance and funeral/burial benefits. The commissioner conducts a **relative value study** used to set reasonable medical charges (subsection numbering has shifted between versions). — [Justia 2011 Utah Code 31A-22-307](https://law.justia.com/codes/utah/2011/title31A/chapter22/section307.html); [FindLaw 31A-22-307](https://codes.findlaw.com/ut/title-31a-insurance-code/ut-code-sect-31a-22-307); [Parker & McConkie](https://www.parkerandmcconkie.com/blog/utahs-3000-pip-minimum-why-most-salt-lake-car-accident-victims-face-massive-medical-bills/)
- 31A-22-309(1): no suit for general damages unless one of: death, dismemberment, permanent disability or impairment based on objective findings, permanent disfigurement, bone fracture, or **medical expenses over $3,000**. — [le.utah.gov 31A-22-309 (eff. 1/1/2021, superseded 5/6/2026)](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title31A/Chapter22/C31A-22-S309_2020051220210101.pdf); [BAM Injury Law](https://baminjurylaw.com/blog/utahs-modified-no-fault-system-when-injured-drivers-can-step-outside-pip-and-sue)
- 31A-22-309(5): PIP is overdue if not paid within **30 days** after the insurer receives reasonable proof. If proof covers only part of the claim, that part is overdue 30 days after its proof is received. Interest runs at **1.5% per month** after the due date. If the claimant's suit forces payment of overdue benefits, the insurer also pays reasonable attorney fees. — [le.utah.gov 31A-22-309](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title31A/Chapter22/C31A-22-S309_2020051220210101.pdf); [Prince v. Bear River Mutual (Utah 2002)](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/ut-supreme-court/1328890.html)
- 31A-22-309(6) covers PIP insurer reimbursement from the at-fault party's insurer, including written notice of the reimbursement amount needed to settle a third-party claim. The PIP carrier is said to have a 3-year window from the date of loss (disputed). — [MWL, Utah PIP subrogation deadline](https://www.mwl-law.com/understanding-utahs-pip-subrogation-deadline-clearing-up-the-confusion-before-time-runs-out/)
- 2026: 31A-22-309 was amended by ch. 382 (2026 GS). The prior version is marked "superseded 5/6/2026". HB 119 (2026, 2nd Sub.) would bar at-fault insurers from reducing compensation because the injured party lacked PIP; enactment not confirmed. One billing source says a 2026 amendment added a rideshare-related exclusion without changing the overdue, interest, attorney-fee or $3,000-threshold provisions. — [le.utah.gov superseded 309 PDF](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title31A/Chapter22/C31A-22-S309_2020051220210101.pdf); [HB 119 2S](https://le.utah.gov/Session/2026/bills/amended/AV_HB0119S02_2026-02-17_09-34-45.pdf); [UID 2026 summary](https://insurance.utah.gov/consumers/legal-resources/legislation/2026-summary/); [AC Billing Utah](https://acbilling.com/chiropractic-billing-utah/)
- Utah has no state-mandated auto/PIP medical fee schedule as of 2025–2026 (secondary). — [AC Billing Utah](https://acbilling.com/chiropractic-billing-utah/)
- Utah has a statewide lien statute (Title 38, ch. 7) per the MWL chart. Its coverage of chiropractors was not confirmed. — [MWL chart](https://www.mwl-law.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/HOSPITAL-LIEN-LAWS-IN-ALL-50-STATES-CHART-00215685x9EBBF.pdf)

### Inferences
