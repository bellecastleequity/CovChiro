Booking & Scheduling

How do I book office coverage?

Enter your clinic's address, choose the number of full days, optionally add a half day, and pick a start date. The calculator shows your region, mileage, and total live, including the 10% deposit due at booking.

How do I book 3.5 days of coverage?

Enter 3 in the Full days field and check "Add a half day" — the half day is added right after your full days, back to back, and priced at the half-day rate for your region.

What counts as a half day vs a full day?

A half day covers up to 4 hours; a full day covers up to 8 hours of clinical coverage. A full day includes one meal break of up to 60 minutes — time beyond that is billed at $100/hour, same as additional coverage beyond the booked hours.

Is coverage confirmed instantly?

Yes — office coverage is confirmed the moment your 10% deposit is processed, with no waiting for a callback.

What if I find out overnight that I need coverage first thing in the morning?

Bookings are monitored 24/7, not just during business hours. If your associate calls out sick at midnight and you need someone at 8am, book it as soon as you know — that request gets an immediate response, the same as any other same-day booking, and it also qualifies for the automatic last-minute discount.

Is there a discount for booking on short notice?

Yes — any coverage date still open the same day or the day before automatically gets 10% off that date's rate. It's applied when you build your quote, no code needed, and only affects the specific date that qualifies if you're booking multiple dates together.

Can I book coverage if I just need extra hands, not full coverage?

Yes. Office coverage is also used by practices that are in the office but need an additional doctor for a high-volume day, week, or month — you don't have to be away to book it.

Pricing & Regions

How are rates determined?

Rates are set by region — North, Central, or South Florida — based on your clinic's address. The map and rate table update automatically once you enter your address.

How is mileage calculated?

Mileage is calculated automatically from your clinic's exact address to the provider's base location in Orlando (32801), at a tiered per-mile rate.

Is a deposit required?

Yes — a 10% deposit is charged by credit card at the time of booking. The remaining balance is invoiced after your coverage is complete.

Balance, Invoicing & Cancellation

When is my remaining balance due?

Once your coverage is marked complete, you'll receive an invoice for the remaining balance, payable by credit card via the invoice or directly in your account portal. Reminders are sent up to twice daily until it's paid in full.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel 48 or more hours before your coverage start date for a full refund. Cancelling inside 48 hours forfeits the deposit portion.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open the booking in your account dashboard and select Cancel — you'll see exactly what's refundable before confirming.

Your Account & Documents

What's in the Documents & forms tab?

Once you have an office coverage or event booking, your dashboard shows a Documents tab with a signed agreement copy, W-9 request, certificate of insurance request, the malpractice declaration page, and coverage guidelines.

Can I request a video interview with the doctor?

Yes, once you have a booking — a "Request video interview" option appears on your booking in the dashboard. These are 10-minute calls, Monday–Friday, 12:00–1:00pm, confirmed by email.

How do I leave a review?

Once a booking is paid in full — including the remaining balance for office coverage — a "Leave a review" option appears on that booking in your dashboard.

Can I specify dress code or techniques I'd like followed?

Yes. The booking form includes a Coverage details section covering dress code, techniques (Diversified, Gonstead, Thompson, Spinal Decompression, Manual Therapy, Ultrasound, E-Stim), any other notes, and a day-of point of contact.
