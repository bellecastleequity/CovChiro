Pre-Arrival Checklist

A few things ready before Dr. McPherson arrives make the day go smoothly for everyone.

  • EHR / practice management system login ready for him to use during the visit
  • Full day's patient schedule confirmed and accessible (printed or on-screen)
  • Any specific treatment protocols, technique preferences, or notes shared through the Coverage Details section of your booking — double-check they still reflect what you need
  • Adjusting tables, modalities, and any equipment he'll need set up and in working order
  • Front desk and clinical staff briefed — the Staff Handout (in your account's Resources tab) covers what they need to know
  • Building access sorted: door codes, parking instructions, where to check in on arrival
  • A point of contact identified for the day, in case anything comes up (this can be filled in on the booking form)
  • Dress code communicated, if your practice has a specific preference beyond the default
