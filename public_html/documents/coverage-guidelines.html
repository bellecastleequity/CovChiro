Coverage Guidelines & Resources

A quick reference for what to expect on a covered day, plus answers to the tax and liability questions that come up most.

What to expect on the day of coverage

  • Dr. McPherson arrives within the billable coverage window booked for that date — not your practice's full posted hours unless those were specifically booked.
  • He follows your practice's existing protocols, technique preferences, and documentation style, noted in the Coverage Details section of your booking.
  • A brief early arrival or late departure is a courtesy, not billed in either direction. Time genuinely needed beyond the booked window (prep, meetings, charting) should be booked as additional hours in advance.
  • Patients are seen and routed exactly as on any normal day — no special instructions needed for your front desk beyond the Staff Handout (also in your Resources tab).

Billing & payment

  • A 10% deposit confirms the booking. The remaining balance is invoiced once coverage is marked complete, and can be paid directly from your dashboard.
  • Payments are made to Key Global LLC — the entity Dr. McPherson operates under for coverage agreements and invoicing.
  • Insurance is billed under your practice as usual; nothing changes on the patient side.

Tax questions

  • Coverage is provided as an independent contractor, not an employee — a completed W-9 is available for your 1099 records in the Resources tab.
  • Payments to Key Global LLC should be recorded the same way you'd record any other contracted professional service.

Liability & insurance

  • Dr. McPherson holds a current, valid, unrestricted chiropractic license and maintains malpractice insurance — both available as instant downloads in your Resources tab.
  • Coverage is performed under an independent contractor relationship — see your signed coverage agreement for the full terms.
