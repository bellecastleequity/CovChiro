What "InjuryCase Certified" means

  • The practice manages its personal injury cases on InjuryCase with an active, paid account.
  • It has a current HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with InjuryCase.
  • Every team member signs in with two-factor authentication, and case data is encrypted and backed up daily.

The badge describes how the practice manages case records. It is not an endorsement of any provider's clinical care, licensure or qualifications, and it says nothing about the outcome of any claim. Status is checked live each time this page loads.
