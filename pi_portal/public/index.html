FAQ

Questions

Is InjuryCase HIPAA compliant?

InjuryCase provides the technical safeguards HIPAA requires (encryption, unique logins with two-factor, automatic logoff, audit logs, access controls) and signs a Business Associate Agreement with your practice. Compliance is shared: your practice still keeps its own policies, training and risk assessment.

Can you import my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. Send us your tracking sheet and A/R sheet as CSV files. We import cases, referrals, checklists, balances, attorneys and insurers, and flag anything that needs a second look.

Does InjuryCase tell us how to treat patients?

No. The care guide, timelines and suggestions are educational prompts based on common PI practice. The treating provider makes every clinical decision (exams, diagnosis, treatment, referrals, imaging and discharge) using their own judgment and discretion for each patient. Prompts can be skipped or marked "not needed" at any time.

Does it work outside Florida?

Yes. The care workflow works in every state, and each case follows its own state's rules: PIP limits and deadlines, lawsuit thresholds, billing limits and provider lien steps. Florida is fully supported; more than 20 other states are configured, and the rest use general at-fault-state rules. Each state's playbook page shows what's different.

Is this an EHR or billing system?

No. InjuryCase sits alongside your EHR and billing software and manages the personal-injury side of each case: attorneys, referrals, deadlines, liens and settlements.

What happens if I cancel?

Your account becomes read-only. You can still view and export every record, and you can resubscribe at any time to pick up where you left off.
