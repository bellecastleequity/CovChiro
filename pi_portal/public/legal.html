Business Associate Agreement

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is between the subscribing practice ("Covered Entity") and the operator of InjuryCase ("Business Associate") and is effective when Covered Entity accepts it in the Service. Version: 2026-10.

1. Permitted uses and disclosures

Business Associate may use and disclose protected health information ("PHI") only to provide the Service to Covered Entity, as required by law, or for its proper management and administration as permitted by 45 CFR §164.504(e)(4).

2. Safeguards

Business Associate will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 (the Security Rule), to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided by this BAA.

3. Reporting

Business Associate will report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not provided for by this BAA, any security incident, and any breach of unsecured PHI as required by 45 CFR §164.410, without unreasonable delay and no later than [__] days after discovery.

4. Subcontractors

Business Associate will ensure that any subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on its behalf (including its hosting provider) agrees in writing to the same restrictions and conditions.

5. Individual rights

Business Associate will make PHI available to Covered Entity as needed for Covered Entity to meet its obligations for access (§164.524), amendment (§164.526) and accounting of disclosures (§164.528).

6. Books and records

Business Associate will make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of HHS for determining compliance.

7. Term and termination

This BAA lasts as long as Business Associate maintains PHI for Covered Entity. Covered Entity may terminate if Business Associate materially breaches it and does not cure.

8. Return or destruction

On termination, Business Associate will return (by export) or destroy all PHI, retaining no copies, or, where infeasible, extend these protections and limit further use. [Retention and destruction timeline — attorney to specify.]

9. Minimum necessary

Business Associate's personnel do not access PHI except as necessary to provide support requested by Covered Entity; platform operator accounts are technically prevented from viewing patient records.
