Capitalized terms not defined here (including Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Electronic Protected Health Information, Individual, Protected Health Information ("PHI"), Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor and Unsecured PHI) have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. "HIPAA Rules" means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification and Enforcement Rules, as amended by the HITECH Act.

2. Permitted uses and disclosures

Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only: (a) to provide the Service to Covered Entity as described in the Terms, including sending communications Covered Entity directs through the Service; (b) as Required by Law; and (c) for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, as permitted by 45 CFR §164.504(e)(4), provided any disclosure is Required by Law or made with reasonable written assurances of confidentiality from the recipient. Business Associate will not use or disclose PHI in a way that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, will not sell PHI, and will not use PHI for marketing. Business Associate will not de-identify PHI or perform Data Aggregation for others without Covered Entity's written permission.

3. Minimum necessary

Business Associate will limit its uses, disclosures and requests of PHI to the minimum necessary. Its personnel do not access PHI except as necessary to provide support Covered Entity requests or to keep the Service secure and running; platform operator accounts are technically prevented from viewing patient records.

4. Safeguards

Business Associate will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 (the Security Rule) with respect to Electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided by this BAA.

5. Reporting

Business Associate will report to Covered Entity: (a) any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA of which it becomes aware; (b) any Security Incident of which it becomes aware; and (c) any Breach of Unsecured PHI, as required by 45 CFR §164.410. Reports will be made without unreasonable delay and in no case later than [10] business days after discovery, and will include, to the extent available, the identity of each Individual affected and the other information Covered Entity needs to give notice under 45 CFR §164.404. The parties agree that this section is notice of the ongoing existence of unsuccessful Security Incidents (such as pings, port scans and blocked sign-in attempts) that do not result in unauthorized access to PHI, for which no further report is required. Business Associate will cooperate with Covered Entity's investigation and mitigation, and will mitigate, to the extent practicable, harmful effects known to it.

6. Subcontractors

Business Associate will ensure, in accordance with 45 CFR §§164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on its behalf (including its hosting provider) agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate. Current subcontractors are listed under Subprocessors.

7. Individual rights

Within 15 business days of Covered Entity's request, Business Associate will make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity so it can meet its obligations for access (§164.524), amendment (§164.526) and an accounting of disclosures (§164.528). Most of this can be done by Covered Entity directly in the Service (records, exports and the audit log). If an Individual asks Business Associate directly, Business Associate will forward the request to Covered Entity.

8. Covered Entity's obligations

Covered Entity will: notify Business Associate of limitations in its Notice of Privacy Practices, changes in an Individual's permission, or restrictions it agrees to, to the extent they affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI; not ask Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in a way not permitted for Covered Entity; and obtain any authorization needed before directing disclosures through the Service (for example, to an attorney).

9. Books and records

Business Associate will make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

10. Term and termination

This BAA starts on its effective date and lasts as long as Business Associate maintains PHI for Covered Entity. If either party knows of a pattern of activity or practice of the other that is a material breach of this BAA, it will give notice and an opportunity to cure within 30 days; if the breach is not cured, the non-breaching party may terminate the Terms and this BAA.

11. Return or destruction of PHI

When the Terms end, Covered Entity may export its PHI from the Service during the read-only period described in the Terms (12 months). Business Associate will then destroy all PHI it maintains, after at least 30 days' notice, and will confirm destruction in writing on request; Covered Entity may ask for earlier destruction. PHI in encrypted backups is destroyed as the backups expire, within 35 days. If return or destruction is infeasible for any PHI, Business Associate will extend the protections of this BAA to it and limit further uses and disclosures to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible.

12. Miscellaneous

References to the HIPAA Rules mean them as in effect or amended. The parties will amend this BAA as needed to comply with changes in the law. Any ambiguity will be resolved to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules. Nothing in this BAA gives rights to any third party. Business Associate's obligations under sections 6 and 11 survive termination.
