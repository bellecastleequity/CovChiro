# Priority firms: Morgan & Morgan and Dan Newlin (Ocala)

`priority-firms.csv` lists the Ocala attorneys named in public directories, plus a row for each firm's case
managers (the people who actually tell clients where to get treated).

## Email addresses: verify, don't guess-and-send
- **Morgan & Morgan:** the pattern sites disagree. ContactOut says first initial + last name (`aoliveri@forthepeople.com`). MailMo says `first.last@forthepeople.com`. Your experience points to the first version.
- **Dan Newlin:** a third-party directory shows `first.last@newlinlaw.com`.
- The guesses sit in a column named **email_guess** on purpose. The Outreach import reads only the **email** column, so nothing guessed is sent until you move a confirmed address over.
- **How to confirm:**
  - **Florida Bar member directory** (floridabar.org → Find a Lawyer). Every Florida attorney's official email is public there. It's the most reliable source, and it shows which pattern the firm really uses.
  - **The front desk.** Ask: "What's the best email for your PIP case manager?"
  - **Your own inbox.** Any email you've had from their staff confirms the pattern.
- Bounces from guessed addresses damage your sending domain's reputation, and a run of them can get a SendGrid account suspended.

## Who to reach at these firms
At big PI firms, intake and pre-suit **case managers**, **medical coordinators** and **paralegals** steer clients to treatment far more than trial attorneys do. Directories don't list them. Get them by calling the office or stopping by, and through LinkedIn ("Morgan & Morgan Ocala case manager").

Pitch to big firms: same/next-day visits inside the 14-day window, clean PIP documentation, fast records with a release, and Spanish/Creole patients welcome. Nothing of value for referrals (§817.505 / §456.054).
