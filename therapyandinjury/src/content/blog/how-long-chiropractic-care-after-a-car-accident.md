It's one of the first questions people ask after a crash, and a fair one. The honest answer: it depends on you, and a good plan says so up front.

## What shapes the length of care

- **The injury itself.** A mild strain and an injury involving several areas of the spine are different situations.
- **How soon you were seen.** Getting evaluated early gives a clearer starting point. In Florida, PIP also generally requires initial care within 14 days of the accident.
- **Your health before the crash.** Earlier injuries, desk-heavy work, and general fitness all play a part.
- **Your daily demands.** A job that involves lifting or long drives may affect how symptoms behave between visits.
- **The home program.** Exercises and habit changes between visits are part of the plan.

## How progress is checked

At Therapy & Injury, your chiropractic physician sets goals with you at the start (for example, turning your head to check a blind spot, or sleeping through the night), and rechecks them as care goes on. The plan changes based on how you're actually doing, not a fixed number of visits.

If progress stalls, or anything suggests you need a medical evaluation or imaging, we refer you to an independent medical provider.

## A word of caution

Be wary of anyone who promises a specific result or recovery timeline before examining you. Every person and every injury is different, and individual results vary.
