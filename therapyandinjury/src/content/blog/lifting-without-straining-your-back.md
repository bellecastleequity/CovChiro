Back strain often comes from ordinary lifts done in a hurry. These habits take a second and can make a difference.

1. **Get close.** Stand close to the object with your feet about hip-width apart.
2. **Bend at the hips and knees.** Let your legs do the work rather than rounding your back.
3. **Hold it close.** The farther a load is from your body, the heavier it feels to your back.
4. **Turn with your feet.** Step to turn instead of twisting your torso while holding something heavy.
5. **Ask for help or split the load.** Two trips beat one strain.

## Signs to get checked

Ongoing back pain after a lift is worth having evaluated. Get medical care right away if back pain comes with numbness or weakness in the legs, loss of bladder or bowel control, or fever.
