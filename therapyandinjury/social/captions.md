## 03 · After a car accident: a calm checklist — `posts/03-after-accident-checklist.html`
Car accidents are stressful. Save this calm checklist for later:
1️⃣ Check for injuries. Call 911 if anyone is hurt.
2️⃣ Move to safety and call the police.
3️⃣ Exchange info and take photos.
4️⃣ Notify your auto insurer.
5️⃣ Get checked within 14 days. Florida PIP generally requires initial care within 14 days of the accident.

Share it with a new driver in your life. 💙

`#DriveSafe #FloridaDrivers #CarAccident #FloridaPIP #{City}FL #Chiropractic`

## 04 · The 14-day PIP reminder — `posts/04-14-day-pip.html`
📅 14 days. That’s the window to remember.

Florida’s no-fault law generally requires that you receive initial care within 14 days of an auto accident for your Personal Injury Protection (PIP) benefits to apply. Some people feel fine at first and notice stiffness or soreness later.

Your policy and insurer determine your coverage. Learn more at the link in bio.

`#FloridaPIP #NoFault #FloridaDrivers #CarAccident #{City}FL #Chiropractic`

## 07 · Is your headrest set right? — `posts/07-headrest-tip.html`
A 10-second car check worth doing today 🚗
• Raise the headrest so the top is about level with the top of your head.
• Keep it as close to the back of your head as is comfortable.
• Re-check whenever someone else drives your car.

## 08 · A friendlier desk setup — `posts/08-desk-posture-tip.html`
Desk day? Try this:
• Screen at about eye level, an arm’s length away

• Elbows close, shoulders relaxed
