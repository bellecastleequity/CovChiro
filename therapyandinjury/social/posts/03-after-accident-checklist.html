After a car accident

A calm checklist

  1. 1Check for injuries. Call 911 if anyone is hurt.
  2. 2Move to safety and call the police.
  3. 3Exchange info and take photos.
  4. 4Notify your auto insurer.
  5. 5Get checked within 14 days.Florida PIP generally requires initial care within 14 days.
