Posture tip

A friendlier desk setup

  1. 1Screen at about eye level, an arm’s length away.
  2. 2Feet flat, hips slightly higher than knees.
  3. 3Elbows close to your sides, shoulders relaxed.
  4. 4Stand up and move a little every half hour or so.
