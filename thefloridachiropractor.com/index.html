Frequently asked

Home visits & event coverage, answered

What is a chiropractic home visit?

A licensed Doctor of Chiropractic comes directly to your home to provide an adjustment — no need to travel if you're managing pain, mobility limits, or simply prefer care in your own space. Book online, get an instant quote, and confirm with a deposit.

How much does a home visit cost?

$100 for the first adult, $70 for each additional adult or child seen in the same visit, plus a flat $0.20/mile one-way travel fee based on your address. Your exact total is calculated live before you book.

Do you offer chiropractic coverage for sporting events or corporate wellness days?

Yes — on-site chiropractic care for sporting events, corporate wellness days, and similar gatherings, billed hourly at $100/hour with a 2-hour minimum. Great for race finish lines, tournaments, employee wellness days, and similar events.

What areas of Florida do you cover for home visits and events?

Home visits and event coverage are available statewide, with mileage calculated automatically from your address.

How does booking and payment work?

Pick your date, get an instant quote, and confirm with a 10% deposit due at booking — the rest is invoiced after your visit or event. Booking is confirmed immediately, with a signed agreement completed as part of checkout.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel 48+ hours before your scheduled time for a full refund of your deposit. Inside 48 hours, the deposit is non-refundable.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — licensed and insured to practice in Florida and the U.S. Virgin Islands, with malpractice coverage carried. License and insurance information is available on request.

Do you also cover chiropractic offices, not just home visits?

Yes. Office coverage for practice owners — vacation coverage, temporary associate support, and extra hands for high patient volume — is booked separately at coveragechiropractor.com.
